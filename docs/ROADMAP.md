# BEH4VIOR: roadmap from the owner

Recorded from the owner's words on 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-11. This is direction, not a build order: nothing here is started unless TASK_QUEUE.md says so. — signed: Claude (Fable 5.2) @ claude.ai chat · 2026-11-11 00:15 EST

## How the owner wants this run

- Start with his own son, as soon as it is online. Build slowly from real use.
- Keep spending low: small steps, no big builds on speculation.
- If it works at home and the school approves and starts using it, push further.
- Keep notes of everything in GitHub and Drive.

## Stage 1: the tracker (built, waiting on deployment)

The school sheet becomes five good things for the next day, 20% each. Sign-in through TAKATAK-V1 at beh4vior.takatak.ca.

## Stage 2: learning games

- Tracing letters with a finger (B, C, D…). Available even after a bad day, in a separate educational side of the app.
- Counting: fish fall from the sky worth +1 to +4; tap to reach exactly 20; going over restarts.
- Puzzles and matching.
- A set per age group. Levels.
- A maximum per day (and per week). The goal is a set amount of learning time, not more phone time.

## Stage 3: courses (learning to talk and write)

Inspired by reading-tutor apps the owner looked at (a child reads aloud, the app listens and helps; a parent sees progress). Start much smaller than those products and grow. Per-child progress page for the parent.

## Stage 4: paid plans

- Parent subscription, billed through TAKATAK-V1 (V1 owns billing).
- The owner's idea: extra levels can be bought when the weekly maximum is reached.

Notes for whoever builds this:
- Buying past the weekly limit works against the limit's own purpose. Ask the owner whether extra levels should mean more variety inside the same time limit, with the time limit staying a parent setting.
- Purchases must be made by the parent, behind the parent's sign-in, never by a tap inside a child's game. App stores and consumer law have rules on purchases in children's apps; get them checked before building.

## Stage 5: parent controls for other apps

The owner would like a parent to control the child's device from the parent's phone (for example time on Roblox and YouTube).

Note: a website cannot control other apps on a phone. This needs a native app using the phone's own parental-control features (Android and iOS each have their own, with store approval). The owner has a separate Android app-locking project; that is the natural home for this, linked to BEH4VIOR, not a feature of this web app.

## Stage 6: schools

Teacher accounts, parent and teacher messages, scanning the paper sheet. Only after the first school agrees, and after the consent and privacy gate in TASK_QUEUE.md.
